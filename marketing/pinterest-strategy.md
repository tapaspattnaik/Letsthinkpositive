# Pinterest Strategy — LetsThinkPositive.com

> Goal: turn Pinterest into the #1 organic referral channel within 6 months.
> Why Pinterest: 70%+ of wellness/self-care searchers on Pinterest are actively *planning* a habit change — the highest-intent free traffic available for this niche. Pins compound for months (unlike IG/TikTok posts that die in 48 hours).

---

## 1. Account Setup

- **Account type:** Business account (free) → unlocks analytics + rich pins
- **Name:** `Lets Think Positive | Daily Positivity & Wellness Tools`
  (keyword-stuffed display names rank in Pinterest search — "positivity", "wellness" are the money words)
- **Bio:** "Free tools to build a calmer, happier you 🌿 Mood tracker · gratitude journal · breathing exercises · 30-day challenges. Start free → letsthinkpositive.com"
- **Claim the website** (Settings → Claimed accounts) — this attributes every pin of your content to you and unlocks website analytics
- **Enable Rich Pins** — auto-pulls title/description from your blog's OG tags (already present in the Next.js app)

## 2. Board Architecture (10 boards)

| Board | Keywords targeted | Pin sources |
|---|---|---|
| Daily Positive Affirmations | affirmations, positive affirmations daily | Affirmation tool screenshots, quote cards |
| Gratitude Journal Prompts | gratitude prompts, journal prompts | Journal feature + blog posts |
| 30-Day Wellness Challenges | 30 day challenge, self improvement challenge | Challenge screenshots, printable trackers |
| Calm & Anxiety Relief | anxiety relief, calming techniques | Breathing tool, sounds, reframe tool |
| Better Sleep Tips | sleep hygiene, sleep tips, can't sleep | Sleep tracker + sleep reset challenge |
| Morning Routine Ideas | morning routine, daily intention | Intention tool, habit content |
| Mindfulness for Beginners | mindfulness, meditation for beginners | Meditation/yoga tools |
| Positive Quotes to Live By | positive quotes, motivational quotes | Quote-card generator output |
| Self-Care Sunday Ideas | self care ideas, self care checklist | Tool roundups |
| Kids Wellness & Calm | kids mindfulness, calm down corner | Kids Zone feature |

**Rule:** every board description = 2–3 sentences with 3+ keywords, ending with the site link.

## 3. Pin Design System

- **Size:** 1000×1500px (2:3) — non-negotiable, Pinterest demotes other ratios
- **Brand kit:** teal `#1A6B6B` / ivory `#FFFCF5` / amber `#E8A020` — match the site exactly so the click-through feels seamless
- **Template types (rotate):**
  1. Text-overlay quote card (large serif headline)
  2. Numbered-list pin ("5 prompts to…") — highest save rate
  3. App screenshot in phone mockup ("Track your mood free")
  4. Before/after or checklist pin
- **Tools:** Canva free tier covers all 4 templates. Make 4 master templates once, then it's 5 min/pin.
- Put the domain `letsthinkpositive.com` in small text at the bottom of EVERY pin — pins get re-shared without attribution otherwise.

## 4. Publishing Cadence

- **Weeks 1–2:** 2 pins/day (backfill boards so they don't look empty)
- **Ongoing:** 1 new pin/day, 5 days/week — sustainable solo cadence
- Schedule natively with Pinterest's built-in scheduler (free, 30 days ahead) — batch one hour every Sunday
- **Re-pin rule:** each blog post gets 3–4 different pin designs over 6 weeks (Pinterest treats each image as fresh content)

## 5. Funnel

```
Pin (problem/curiosity) → Blog post or tool landing page → CTA: "Try free, no signup needed for X" → Signup for streaks/saving
```

Best Pinterest landing pages from the current site:
- `/quotes` (instant gratification, shareable output)
- `/breathing` (works without login)
- `/blog/*` posts (SEO content – see seo-blog-outlines.md)
- `/challenges` (matches "30 day challenge" search intent)

## 6. Measurement (monthly)

- Pinterest Analytics: impressions, saves (the leading indicator), outbound clicks
- GA/Umami: sessions from pinterest.com, signup conversions
- Kill criteria: any board with <1% save rate after 2 months → merge or re-keyword
- Target: 10k monthly impressions by month 2, 50k by month 6, 3–5% outbound click rate
