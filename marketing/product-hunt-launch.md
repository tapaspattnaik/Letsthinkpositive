## 2. Launch Strategy

### When
- **Tuesday or Wednesday, 12:01 AM Pacific.** Mon/Fri have weaker traffic; Tue–Wed have the most hunters AND the most competition — for a community-driven product, traffic wins.
- Avoid: holidays, days when a major AI company is rumoured to launch.

### Hunter
- Self-hunt is fine in 2025+ — hunter follower notifications were removed; a famous hunter adds credibility but not distribution. Don't block the launch waiting for one.

### The first 4 hours decide everything
PH's algorithm weights early velocity. Line up (DO NOT ask for upvotes explicitly — against PH rules; ask people to "check it out and share honest feedback"):
- Personal WhatsApp/Telegram contacts (the warmest list)
- The app's existing users — banner on the site + email (see template below)
- LinkedIn + X post at 7am IST (PH day is already underway)
- Relevant Slack/Discord communities you're genuinely a member of

### Launch-day email to existing users

```
Subject: We're live on Product Hunt today 🌿 (a tiny favour)

Hi {name},

Today's a big day — Lets Think Positive is live on Product Hunt!

If the app has helped your days feel a little lighter, would you take 60
seconds to check out our launch and leave an honest comment about your
experience? Real stories from real users mean everything.

→ [Product Hunt link]

Whatever happens today, thank you for being part of this 💛

— Tapas
```

### On-site launch banner (add to layout for 24h)
> 🚀 We're live on Product Hunt today! If LTP helps you, [come say hi →]

## 3. Asset & Prep Checklist

### T-minus 2 weeks
- [ ] Create PH account if needed; complete profile (photo, bio, links)
- [ ] Be active on PH daily: upvote, comment genuinely (warm account > cold account)
- [ ] Set up the "Coming Soon" teaser page on PH (collects followers who get notified at launch)
- [ ] Draft all copy above into the PH submission form (save as draft)
- [ ] Book the launch date in calendar + clear that day of other work

### T-minus 1 week
- [ ] Produce all 7–8 gallery assets (Canva/Figma, brand colors)
- [ ] Record the 30s demo video (optional but +15–20% conversion on PH)
- [ ] Test the ENTIRE first-visit flow as a stranger: onboarding, signup, first mood log — fix any friction
- [ ] Check site handles traffic: Hostinger plan limits, image optimisation
- [ ] **Run `npx prisma db push` on Hostinger** (weekly_insights table — still pending!) so AI insights work for new users
- [ ] Add UTM links: `?utm_source=producthunt&utm_campaign=launch`
- [ ] Prepare launch-day social posts (LinkedIn, X, Instagram story)
- [ ] Write the launch-day email in your ESP, scheduled for 7am IST

### T-minus 1 day
- [ ] Final submission review; schedule for 12:01 AM PT
- [ ] Message your warm list: "launching tomorrow, would love your honest take"
- [ ] Sleep early — launch day is a 16-hour shift

### Launch day
- [ ] 12:05 AM PT: post the maker comment
- [ ] Reply to EVERY comment within 30 min, all day (PH rewards engagement)
- [ ] 7 AM IST: site banner live, email sent, LinkedIn/X posts up
- [ ] Midday: share milestone update ("#5 in Health & Fitness — thank you!")
- [ ] Track signups via UTM in analytics
- [ ] Evening: thank-you post regardless of rank

### T-plus 1 week
- [ ] Add PH badge to site footer (featured/top-5 badge if earned)
- [ ] Thank-you email to users with results + what's next
- [ ] Write a "What we learned launching on PH" blog post (SEO + community goodwill)
- [ ] Follow up with every commenter who suggested a feature

## 4. Realistic Goals

| Metric | Conservative | Good day | Dream |
|---|---|---|---|
| Upvotes | 80–150 | 300–500 | 800+ (front page) |
| Comments | 15–30 | 50–80 | 120+ |
| Site visits | 500–1,500 | 3,000–6,000 | 15,000+ |
| Signups | 50–150 | 300–600 | 1,500+ |

The durable wins either way: a permanent dofollow backlink (domain authority), the badge for social proof, and the launch-week cohort of feedback-givers.
